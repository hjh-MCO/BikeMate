A hookless rim has a straight inner wall with no lip locking the tire bead in place, unlike traditional hooked rims. This is common on modern tubeless-ready carbon wheels. Because there's no mechanical hook holding the bead, hookless rims carry a lower maximum safe pressure — commonly cited around 72.5 PSI — regardless of what your tire or rim's own printed limits say. Check your specific rim manufacturer's rated maximum to be sure.
